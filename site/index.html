CAPM → Fama-French 3/4/5 factor exposure, out-of-sample validation, and a cost-aware momentum-tilt backtest across a 16-stock US universe · 2015-2023 · monthly

Out-of-sample validation

Betas estimated on 2015-2020 only, applied once to 2021-2023. OOS R² is measured against a train-mean benchmark - above zero means the factor structure generalized.

Backtest: 12-1 momentum tilt, 2021-2023

Each month: rank the 16 stocks by trailing 12-1 momentum (strictly past data), hold the top 5 equal-weight, rebalance monthly. Net = 10 bps per unit one-way turnover. Benchmarks: equal-weight universe and SPY.

Method & honest caveats

Pipeline

  • Yahoo Finance auto-adjusted monthly closes; Ken French library factors (MKT, SMB, HML, RMW, CMA, UMD, RF)
  • Excess-return regressions with Newey-West HAC(6); BH control over the 16 alphas per model
  • Residual diagnostics: Jarque-Bera, Ljung-Box(12)
  • Frozen artifacts, sha256 manifest, audit script, pytest suite

What this does NOT claim

  • The 16-stock universe is hindsight-selected (survivorship bias) - backtest returns are optimistic by construction
  • 36 test months is short; no alpha is claimed anywhere
  • The momentum tilt beats SPY on total return but not the equal-weight universe on Sharpe - reported as-is
  • Monthly rebalancing at ~5.7× annual turnover is cost-sensitive; the 50 bps stress shows it
